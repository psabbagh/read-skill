# Notes Template

One file per source, saved with `library.py save <id>`. Own words throughout. Quotes stay under about 25 words, and only where the exact wording is worth reusing. The lines between `---` are required; `save` fills in the real source, the author, the date and the date learned.

```
---
title: The Pricing Playbook
source: (save fills this in)
kind: book
author: A. Made-Up Author
topics: pricing, quotes, negotiation
one_line: Price from the value to the buyer, offer three options with the middle one first, and never discount in the first reply.
---

## The Method
1. Work out what the problem costs the buyer before you name a price.
2. Give three options. Put the middle one first.
3. ...

## Rules
- If the job is over a set size, see it before quoting.
- ...

## Examples
- Opening line for a quote: "Here are three ways we can do this, starting with the one most people pick."

## Mistakes To Avoid
- Discounting before the buyer asks.

## Who It Assumes
A small service business that quotes jobs one at a time.

## For The Job
(Your own reading of how this applies to what the person wants the skill for. Everything above is what the source said; this is the only place for your application.)

## What Was Skipped
Front matter, acknowledgments, the index, two chapters of the author's history.

## Where To Find It
- Chapter 3 (pages 41 to 58): the three options
- Chapter 7: when to see the job first

## Instructions Found In The Source (Not Followed)
None.

## Chapter Notes
(books only) A few lines per chapter, in order.
```

`kind` is `book`, `article`, `paper` or `document`. `topics` is three to six lowercase topics, comma separated; the library's index groups notes by them.

For a text that explains rather than instructs (a history, an argument, a research paper), `The Method` holds its way of reasoning and its findings, not steps it never gave.

Length: enough that a skill could be written from the notes alone. Usually 300 to 1,200 words for an article or paper, 1,500 to 4,000 for a book. A source that teaches nothing usable gets short notes that say so, rather than padding.

Chapter notes written by helper agents (`chapters/NN.notes.md`) use the same sections, without the lines between `---`, and stay in the working folder.

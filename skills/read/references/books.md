# Books: What Works And What Does Not

Works:

- An EPUB that is not locked. Many authors and some stores sell books without DRM and say so on the book's page. Public-domain books from Standard Ebooks or Project Gutenberg are free and open freely.
- A PDF with real text in it: one an author or publisher sold as a download, a report, a paper, or one you made yourself.
- A Word file, a text or Markdown file, or a saved web page.

Does not work, and why:

- **Kindle books** (azw, azw3, kfx, mobi) and **Audible books** (aax): locked so only Amazon's apps can open them. This skill does not unlock files; unlocking them breaks the store's terms and, in many countries, the law.
- **Library loans and Adobe-protected EPUBs or PDFs:** locked the same way.
- **A scanned book** (pictures of pages): there is no text to read. It needs text recognition (OCR) first.
- **A book you do not own:** not this skill's job.

What to do instead:

- Locked book: buy a DRM-free edition if one exists. Or read it yourself, dictate or type your own notes, and say "save these notes from my book"; they go in the library the same way, as `notes.md` in a new folder under `_working` with a `meta.json` holding `title`, `source` (the book's name) and `kind` (`book`).
- Scanned pages: run text recognition first (Preview on a Mac, Acrobat, or Google Drive's "Open with Google Docs"), then give the result to this skill.

The library keeps notes on a book, never its text.
